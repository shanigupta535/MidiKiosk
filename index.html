Speak once. Your doctor reads everything before you sit down.

MediKiosk registers the patient, asks structured follow-up questions in Hindi or English, reads the old prescription, and delivers a clean clinical history to the doctor — so the consultation begins at the diagnosis, not at the paperwork.
